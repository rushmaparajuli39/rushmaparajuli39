## Featured work

### [Entity Document CRM](https://github.com/rushmaparajuli39/CRM)
**v1 shipped · actively developed** · [Live app](https://crm-app-liard-phi.vercel.app) · [v1 requirements](https://rushmaparajuli39.github.io/requirements/crm-v1.html) · [Source](https://github.com/rushmaparajuli39/CRM)

One place to track EIN records, licenses, and insurance policies (and the documents behind them) for 20 business entities.

- **Access control enforced in the database:** admin, editor, and viewer roles backed by Postgres Row Level Security, not just hidden buttons
- **Nothing expires unnoticed:** license and insurance alerts at 30 and 60 days, plus a monthly check for missing cash sheets with automatic email reminders
- **Built for the field:** staff can take a photo of a document with their phone camera and upload it on the spot
- **Accountable:** database triggers record every create, edit, and delete in an audit log

### Sarathi One
**Planning · requirements stage**

A plan to turn the Entity Document CRM into a multi-tenant SaaS product any business can sign up for, with self-serve sign-up, an isolated workspace for each business, and an installable mobile app (PWA). It will use the same Next.js and Supabase stack, with Row Level Security keeping each business's data separate.
